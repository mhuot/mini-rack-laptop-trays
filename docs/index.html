Mini-Rack Laptop Trays

1U sliding trays for a 10-inch mini rack — MacBook Pro 14 and Surface Laptop 13.8 on smooth rods, with ducted rear exhaust cooling. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Full build details on GitHub.

Front is toward the protruding laptops; the brown blocks are the Noctua exhaust fans.

Printed parts, STLs, and the Fusion 360 scripts that generate this model are in the repository. MIT licensed.
